# VisitorManagementApp
A Visitor Management App is a digital solution used by businesses, schools, hospitals, and residential 
complexes to track, manage, and streamline the process of welcoming guests. 
It replaces traditional paper guestbooks with a secure, automated, and digital sign-in system.

## Core Purposes & Uses
- Streamlined Check-Ins: Visitors can quickly register themselves via a tablet kiosk at the
  front desk or scan a QR code on their own mobile devices for a contactless experience.
- Instant Host Notifications: The moment a guest signs in, the app automatically alerts the employee they are meeting
  via email, text, or app notification, eliminating the need for front-desk staff to track them down.
- Enhanced Security & Verification: The app tracks exactly who is in the building in real time.
  It can capture visitor photos, scan IDs, issue digital or printed access badges, and screen guests against watchlists.
- Emergency Readiness: In the event of an evacuation, administrators can instantly pull up a real-time log of every guest,
  contractor, and employee currently on-site to ensure everyone is accounted for.
